GoreeCloud Search

Privacy and sources

Transparency about what this private VPS Development service does and does not protect.

Which providers receive searches?

All Sources merges Brave Search, Startpage, Qwant, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Bing and Google where category adapters exist. Each selected external provider may receive the query through the required anonymous relay. Ecosia supports Web only and Startpage has no Videos adapter. Blocked engines are reported rather than silently replaced. The experimental native crawler/index remains a separate development-only opt-in.

These external services may retain or process submitted searches and connection metadata under their own privacy policies. This Development service does not promise complete anonymity. Search engines may rate-limit, challenge, or reject requests.

What stays on your device?

The chosen search-provider preference and other preferences are stored in your browser's cookies. There is no GoreeCloud personal profile, hosted AI transmission, or independent search index in this Development build.

Search transport and content

Search terms are submitted by POST instead of being put in the local address-bar URL. Remote autocomplete and anonymous metrics are disabled in this Development profile, and images are proxied. Browser clicks on search results can still send you to third-party sites. Other privacy and security limitations remain.

Development limitations

This Development service is hosted at search-dev.goreecloud.com on a VPS behind NetBird-only HTTPS access. Public clients are denied by the reverse proxy, and the Search containers publish no host ports. External provider requests use a Tor SOCKS5H relay. These measures do not guarantee anonymity or provider availability. Abuse protection, provider compatibility, recovery validation, and the full production security/privacy review are still incomplete.
