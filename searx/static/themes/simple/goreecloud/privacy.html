GoreeCloud Search

Privacy and sources

Transparency about what this local Development build does and does not protect.

Which providers receive searches?

Brave Search, Startpage, and Qwant are the only approved provider families. The default sends search terms to all three, or you can choose one provider in Settings. Web, Images and News have adapters for all three; Videos uses Brave Search and Qwant. A blocked provider is never silently replaced by a different one.

External providers can read the terms you search for, even when the network route masks the Search server's IP address. They can still process queries, timing and metadata under their own privacy policies. Do not enter personal identifiers if you need to keep them from providers.

IP masking and fail-closed search

This private Development version requires a verified Tor SOCKS5h relay for outbound provider and image-proxy requests. It resolves provider names through the relay instead of local DNS and never falls back to direct network traffic. If the relay is missing, unverified or blocked, live searches may be unavailable. Tor is not installed automatically with Search.

Tor routes can hide the server's public IP from providers, but the provider still receives the query itself. Tor exits can be refused or rate-limited; this does not promise complete anonymity, unlinkable searches, or that following result links in your browser will be masked.

What stays on your device?

The chosen search-provider preference and other preferences are stored in your browser's cookies. There is no GoreeCloud personal profile, hosted AI transmission, or independent search index in this Development build.

Search transport and content

Search terms are submitted by POST instead of being put in the local address-bar URL. Remote autocomplete and anonymous metrics are disabled in this local profile, and images are proxied. Browser clicks on search results can still send you to third-party sites. Other privacy and security limitations remain.

Development limitations

The application is bound to loopback for local testing. A public service needs separate authentication, security, abuse protection, privacy review, logging and retention controls. The full public security/privacy audit has not been completed.
