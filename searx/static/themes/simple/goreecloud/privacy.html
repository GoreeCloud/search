Privacy and source disclosure

This Development build of GoreeCloud Search is based on SearXNG. Searching forwards terms to the search engines enabled by the instance operator. Connected providers receive requests from the search server and can retain or use request data under their own policies. The server operator may observe client IP addresses and searches unless separate infrastructure controls protect them. This service does not guarantee anonymity.

In the included loopback-only development profile, search submission defaults to POST, remote autocomplete and metrics are disabled, and images are proxied. Operators may change these settings. An approved public deployment, complete security/privacy review, GoreeCloud Privacy Shield integration, and retention controls have not been established.

No independent GoreeCloud index, hosted AI model transmission, persistent user profile, or personal saved-search service is included in this source import.

Source: GoreeCloud Search and SearXNG. The source is distributed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Do not place secrets or search queries in logs or public reports.
